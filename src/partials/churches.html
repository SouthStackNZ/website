Free for churches

We build it free. You pay for your domain. That's it.

For registered churches and faith communities — the build and hosting are completely free. Your only cost is registering your domain name, paid directly to the registrar. That's typically $15–25 NZD per year for a .co.nz, .org.nz, or .nz domain.

Hosting is on Cloudflare Pages — free tier, fast, and reliable. No monthly fees, no platform subscription, no lock-in. You own the site outright from day one.

What's included: up to 6 pages, CMS so you can update content yourself, events page, contact form, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, mobile responsive, HTTPS, full IP ownership.

Not included: e-commerce, booking systems, or complex integrations — these would be quoted separately.
