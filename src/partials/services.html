How it works

The process

Every project starts with a quick form and a real estimate — then follows the same clear phases through to launch.

01 — ENQUIRY

Two minutes, instant estimate

Fill in our short form and you'll see a starting price on screen straight away. We'll also send you an email with the full breakdown and two ways to go deeper.

02 — DISCOVERY

Call or AI chat — your choice

Book a 20-minute call to talk through your project, or answer a few questions with our AI at your own pace. Either way, we build a complete brief before writing your proposal.

03 — PROPOSAL

Proposal & SOW

You receive a clear written proposal with scope, timeline, and fixed pricing. No surprises. Work only begins once both parties sign and deposit is paid.

04 — DESIGN

Design in Figma

Full site design delivered in Figma for your review and approval. Design is signed off before a single line of code is written.

05 — BUILD

Development

We build the approved design. You get a staging link to review the full site before it goes live.

06 — LAUNCH

Launch & handover

We run a full pre-launch checklist, go live, and hand over everything — including a Loom walkthrough of your CMS. You own the site outright.
