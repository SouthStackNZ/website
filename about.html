About SouthStack

A technical partner, not just a vendor

SouthStack is a web development studio based in Rolleston, Canterbury. We build fast, accessible, NZ-compliant websites and web applications for small to medium businesses across New Zealand.

Founded in 2026 with a clear mission: to build genuinely effective websites for NZ businesses — with clear contracts, honest pricing, and no lock-in.

We're a small studio, which means you work directly with the founder — not an account manager. Every project gets our full attention from first call to final handover.

Our value proposition

"SouthStack builds high-performance websites that load fast, rank well, meet NZ accessibility law, and are built to convert — with transparent pricing, clear IP ownership, and no ongoing lock-in."

What makes us different

Built on principles

🚀

Performance first by default

We choose Astro for content sites because it delivers near-instant load times. Not because it's trendy — because slow websites cost businesses money.

🇳🇿

NZ compliance built in

WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, NZ Privacy Act 2020, GST handling — all included as standard. Not an add-on, not an afterthought.

📄

You own everything

Full IP transfer on final payment. The code, the designs, the content — all yours. No licence fees, no platform lock-in, no ongoing dependency on us.

💬

Direct founder involvement

No account managers, no handoffs to junior developers. You talk directly to the person building your site, from first call to final handover.

📋

Clear contracts upfront

Every project starts with a signed Statement of Work defining exactly what's in scope. No surprises, no scope creep, no unpaid invoices.

📊

Growing data capability

As AWS and data science skills develop, SouthStack can serve clients needing more than a website — pipelines, dashboards, and ML features.

Honest about AI

We use AI. Here's exactly what that means.

We use AI tools — Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and others — to work more efficiently. This is no different to a builder using power tools. The tools don't replace judgement, experience, or accountability.

What AI cannot do: understand your business goals, apply NZ accessibility law correctly, manage a project, take legal responsibility if something goes wrong, or fix it under warranty when it breaks. That requires a person.

✓ What AI helps with

Scaffolding code faster, suggesting refactors, catching errors, writing first drafts of copy.

✗ What AI can't replace

Strategy, NZ legal compliance, client management, accessibility judgement, and accountability.
