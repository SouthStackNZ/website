How it works

The process

Every project follows the same clear phases — no surprises, no scope creep.

01 — DISCOVERY

Discovery call

We talk through your goals, audience, and requirements. We confirm if we're a good fit and what the project needs.

02 — PROPOSAL

Proposal & SOW

You receive a clear written proposal with scope, timeline, and fixed pricing. No surprises. Work only begins once both parties sign and deposit is paid.

03 — DESIGN

Design in Figma

Full site design delivered in Figma for your review and approval. Design is signed off before a single line of code is written.

04 — BUILD

Development

We build the approved design. You get a staging link to review the full site before it goes live.

05 — LAUNCH

Launch & handover

We run a full pre-launch checklist, go live, and hand over everything — including a Loom walkthrough of your CMS. You own the site outright.
